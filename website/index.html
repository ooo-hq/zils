Training & evaluation

A checkpoint earns its place through measured probability quality.

Evaluation contract ↗
  1. 1

    Train

    Miners train compatible adapters and decision heads with a fixed one-epoch recipe and different seeds.

  2. 2

    Submit

    Each miner freezes a candidate and signs its checkpoint hash for the validator.

  3. 3

    Evaluate

    The validator verifies the checkpoint and runs its own evaluation of the model’s probabilities.

  4. 4

    Reward

    Family-macro Brier skill determines proposed weights across eligible candidates.
